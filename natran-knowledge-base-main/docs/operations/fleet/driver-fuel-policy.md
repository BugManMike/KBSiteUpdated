Section 2.4 of the signed Employee Agreement.

# The PIN identifies the person

Authorised employees are issued a **4- to 6-digit PIN** used with the company fuel card. The PIN
authorises fuelling **company vehicles only, with a company gas card**.

**The PIN identifies the employee by name on a weekly fuel report**, and **the employee is
accountable for every transaction made with it.**

- **The PIN may not be shared with anyone.**
- If the employee believes someone else knows it, they **must notify their supervisor
  immediately**.

To issue one, see [Wex fuel card setup](/operations/fleet/wex-fuel-card-setup.md), where the PIN is
created as the **Driver Prompt ID**.

# Cards belong to vehicles, PINs belong to people

Each card is assigned to **an individual vehicle**, or to a **specific fuelling purpose** — for
example an off-road equipment card.

**An employee's PIN works with any company fuel card.** That is what makes the next rule necessary:

> **Employees may not swap or share cards between vehicles**, or use a card for anything other than
> fuelling its intended vehicle or purpose.

Because the PIN travels with the person and the card with the vehicle, the weekly report can show
who fuelled what. Swapping cards breaks that.

# At every fill

- **Completely fill the vehicle's tank.**
- **Enter an accurate odometer reading.**

Both exist so the company can monitor fuel usage **and track required maintenance intervals** —
which is how the mileage-based obligations in
[vehicle use policy](/operations/fleet/vehicle-use-policy.md), such as tire rotation every 10,000
miles, get triggered. An inaccurate odometer entry defeats the maintenance schedule, not just the
fuel report.

# Misuse

**Using the company fuel card for a personal vehicle or any non-business purpose is considered theft
of company funds**, subject to discipline up to and including termination. **Amounts stolen are
deducted from the employee's final paycheck.**
