# Fleet

Company vehicles, fuel cards, and the equipment issued alongside them. The policies here are
signed by the employee as part of the Employee Agreement, but fleet operations own them.

* [Vehicle use policy](vehicle-use-policy.md) - Driver requirements, permitted use, care and maintenance, storage, the accident procedure, and the employee's financial exposure when at fault.
* [Driver fuel policy](driver-fuel-policy.md) - How fuel card PINs identify a driver, what must be entered at every fill, and why cards and PINs may never be shared.
* [Wex fuel card setup](wex-fuel-card-setup.md) - Adding a driver to the Exxon system with a fuel PIN, then inviting them to the Wex mobile app.
* [Equipment and inventory responsibility](equipment-and-inventory-responsibility.md) - The standing payroll-deduction authorisation for lost or negligently damaged equipment, and the prohibition on storing product at home.
* [Budgeting for a vehicle purchase](vehicle-purchase-budgeting.md) - Entering a vehicle into the budget model as an assumption, then moving it to the debt and depreciation schedules after purchase. **Source is a work in progress.**
