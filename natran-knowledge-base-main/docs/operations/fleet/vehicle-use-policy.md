# Driver requirements

Any driver of a company vehicle, or anyone driving on company business, must:

- Possess a **valid driver's licence**
- Maintain an **acceptable driving record** per company policy
- Maintain a score of **90% or better on internal driving safety metrics**
- Follow this policy at all times

> **The 90% metric is the only hard number here and the source does not say what produces it.**
> Telematics is the likely answer — the wiki has an Azuga installation form in its Fleet Management
> folder. Where a driver sees their own score is not documented. Flagged for the operations batch.

Drivers also sign a **motor vehicle record check authorization**, valid for the duration of
employment.

# Permitted use

- **Business purposes only. It may not be used for personal reasons.**
- **Loaning the vehicle is strictly prohibited.**
- **Transporting strangers, hitchhikers, or anyone other than a co-worker** is a violation.
- Use is limited to **transportation to and from job sites and office locations**. Leisure
  excursions and non-work destinations are prohibited.

> **In the event of an accident during such an excursion, the driver accepts all liability
> personally.** That is the sharpest consequence in this policy — insurance cover is effectively
> conditional on the trip being work.

# Care and maintenance

- Vehicles are **non-smoking areas**.
- Interior **and** exterior must be cleaned **at least once a week**.
- **A $125.00 cleaning fee is deducted from the final paycheck** if the interior is not returned in
  well-maintained condition.
- **Initial inspection:** on receiving the vehicle, the employee inspects and notes pre-existing
  damage.
- **Tires:** visually inspect pressure and keep it at the PSI in the manual or on the door panel.
  Report alignment and wheel-balance problems immediately.
- **A written driver safety checklist at least once per month**, inspecting headlights, taillights,
  brake lights, turn signals, running lights, licence plate lighting, tire tread depth, windshield
  wipers and horn.

Scheduled maintenance is the employee's responsibility:

- Oil changed per the manufacturer's schedule
- Tires inflated to proper PSI
- **Tires rotated every 10,000 miles**
- Belts and hoses inspected for cracks, leaks and loose fittings per the manufacturer's schedule

> **Neglect has two consequences:** the company may charge the employee for resulting repairs, and
> unusual wear beyond industry average may mean **loss of the vehicle and/or discipline including
> suspension or termination**.

# Storage

If the vehicle is stored at home:

- **A safe, secure area** — driveway, garage, or fenced secure parking. **Street-side storage is
  prohibited.**
- **Locked and secured** whenever unattended.
- **Above flood levels.**

**The employee is responsible for flood damage, or damage from failure to store securely, up to
$1,000.00, deducted from their paychecks.**

If no suitable parking is available at home, the vehicle **must be stored at the company office** or
another location meeting those criteria.

# Accidents

A **valid insurance card and vehicle registration** must be in the vehicle at all times.

1. **If there is an injury requiring medical attention, call 911.**
2. **Contact your manager immediately** and complete an incident form.
3. If another vehicle is involved, obtain: driver's name (and owner's name if different), address,
   telephone number, insurance company and policy, VIN, vehicle year/make/model, licence plate.
4. **If safe to do so, photograph all vehicles and damage.**
5. **Retain dashcam footage** — contact your manager for help.
6. Obtain names, addresses and telephone numbers of **witnesses**, and the name, badge number,
   department and address of any **investigating officer**.
7. Identify yourself and show licence and insurance card.

**What not to do:**

- **Do not discuss the company's insurance policy.**
- **Do not assume blame.**
- **Above all, do not agree to any settlement.**
- Cooperate with officers; answer factually and **avoid commentary beyond that**.
- **Do not insist a citation be issued to the other driver.** The officer may be deciding
  responsibility, and an overly aggressive attitude may result in a decision against you. If asked
  directly whether you want one issued, answer yes and explain it is company preference.
- Note whether **anyone** reported injuries.

Injuries follow [injury and incident response](/people/injury-and-incident-response.md).

# At-fault accidents

- The company **may require a defensive driving course**, **at the employee's cost**, with proof of
  completion.
- **The employee is financially responsible for the first $1,000.00 of physical damage** to the
  vehicle, deducted from their paychecks.
- The company **may take disciplinary action up to and including termination**.
- The company **may require a drug and/or alcohol test, at the employee's cost**. **If the employee
  is found under the influence at the time of the accident, employment is terminated.**

# Driving infractions

- All vehicles must be **properly maintained and safe**; employees report issues to their supervisor.
- **Alcohol, illegal drugs, or prescription drugs that impair judgment are strictly prohibited** when
  driving on company business.
- **Handheld device use while driving is prohibited.** Hands-free is permitted for calls, but
  employees are **encouraged to pull over**.
- **Texting while driving is strictly prohibited.**
- Remain focused; avoid texting, eating and other non-essential tasks.
- Obey all traffic laws — speed limits, seat belts, no distracted or impaired driving.

**Discipline** ranges from verbal warning through written warning and suspension to termination,
depending on the nature and frequency of the violation.
