**This document is written for staff, not IT.**

> **Safety first.** If you are driving, pull over and park somewhere safe before reporting your
> issue.

# Where to report

1. Post your issue in the **IT Support** group chat.
2. **If Chat or your Google account is the problem**, contact your manager so they can report it
   on your behalf.
3. **If someone has already reported the same issue**, use **reply in thread** on their original
   message rather than starting a new one. This keeps issues organised.
4. If support asks follow-up questions, respond promptly — it is what makes the difference between
   a fast fix and a slow one.

> The source links a specific Google Chat room. There is a second support route in the corpus:
> [Apple device self-service setup](/it/devices/apple-device-self-service-setup.md) tells users to
> call or text `281-377-8227` or email `admin@natran.com`, and says a Google Chat to
> `admin@natran.com` gets the fastest response. **Three channels, two documents, no statement of
> which is preferred.** IT to settle — a user reading only one document will not know the other
> exists.

# What to include

- **A detailed description** of the issue.
- **Whether it is a device or an app problem** — device meaning iPhone, iPad, laptop; app meaning
  Fieldroutes, Gmail, the Gusto app and so on.
- **What happened and what you expected instead**, with step-by-step instructions so support can
  reproduce it.
- **If it affects one customer or appointment**, the customer name, location, customer ID and
  anything else needed to find it.
- **Screenshots or short videos** wherever possible.

> Asking for the customer ID is the useful detail here — it turns "the app is broken" into
> something reproducible.
