Invoices are submitted by email to **`AccountsPayable@Natran.com`**.

That is the whole of it. From there the invoice is
[forwarded into Bill.com](/finance/bill-com-invoice-intake.md) with its GL coding and branch
classification, and [entered as a bill](/finance/bill-com-bill-entry.md).

> This is the entire content of the source document — one sentence. Kept as its own concept because it
> is the answer to a question people actually ask, and it is the entry point to the payables chain
> rather than a step inside it.
