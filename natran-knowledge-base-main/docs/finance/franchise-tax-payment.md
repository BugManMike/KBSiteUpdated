**The accountant files the return; the company makes the payment.** That division is the point of this
procedure — nobody here prepares the franchise tax calculation.

# Steps

1. Sign in to <https://security.app.cpa.state.tx.us/> using company credentials.
2. Select **File and Pay** for Franchise Tax.
3. Select the **Payment Only** method.
4. Select the tax filing period. **Ask the accountant if unsure.**
5. Enter the banking details and submit payment. **Use the Advantage Pro Services, Inc. bank
   account** — payments are made from the APS account but cover **both Natran and APS**.
6. **Retain a copy of the payment receipt and submit it to the bookkeeper** for entry.

> **The source says franchise tax is "a payment made to the state every month".** Texas franchise tax
> is an **annual** tax, with the report and payment due 15 May. A monthly cadence is very likely
> wrong — and it matters, because
> [the accounting schedule of events](/finance/accounting-schedule-of-events.md) is where anyone would
> look to know when this is due. **The accountant should confirm the real frequency.**

> **The payment covers both companies but comes out of one company's account**, with no step recording
> the intercompany portion. [Intercompany transactions](/finance/intercompany-transactions.md) exists
> for exactly this case; whether the franchise tax payment gets that treatment is not stated. Handing
> the receipt to the bookkeeper may be where it happens, but the procedure does not say so.
