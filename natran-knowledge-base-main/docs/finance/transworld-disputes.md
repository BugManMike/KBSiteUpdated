Once an account has been [written off and sent to the agency](/finance/sending-accounts-to-collections.md),
the consumer can dispute the debt. Transworld surfaces those disputes as alerts, and the company has to
substantiate the debt for each one.

# 1. Log in

1. Sign in at <https://my.tsico.com>.
2. You land on the **Dashboard** tab / **Home** page.

# 2. Open the alerts

Scroll to the **Alerts** section → **Upload Document**, top right of that section.

# 3. Work the Consumer Dispute — Collection list

**Every customer in this section needs to be disputed.** For each, the consumer must be provided with:

- **A formal written explanation of the debt** — the signed agreement, notes on the agreement, and
  completed service documents.
- **Full verification of the debt** — invoices, statements, contracts and anything else that details
  and verifies the amount assigned for collection.

**Transworld responds within 3–5 business days** once everything is provided.

Then prepare the letter: **make a copy of the Dispute Letter template** and add

- the customer name and full address
- **all open invoices that were written off** the account in Fieldroutes

# 4. Upload the documents

Back in the **Consumer Dispute — Collection** section in TSI:

1. Select the **green check mark** under **Actions** beside the customer.
2. Upload every document in the pop-up — agreements, the dispute letter, invoices as needed.
3. **Complete.**

**If the customer has no signed agreement, use the completed service document in its place.**

> **The fallback in the last line is the substantive risk here.** A completed service document proves
> service was performed; it does not prove the customer agreed to the price or the term. Substituting
> one for the other is exactly the weakness a consumer dispute is testing, and the same gap sinks a
> [chargeback rebuttal](/finance/chargeback-disputes.md), which relies on the signed agreement.
> **How often accounts reach collections without a signed agreement is worth knowing** — and if it is
> common, the problem is at sign-up, not here.

> The agency appears as both **Transworld** and **TSI** in the same document, and the portal is
> `tsico.com`. Same organisation. Batch 4a flagged a separate unresolved question — **whether debt goes
> to TSI or to ARM** — which this document does not settle.
