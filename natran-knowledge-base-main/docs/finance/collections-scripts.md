**Which script depends on one check.** Open the **Invoices** tab on the customer card. **If the last
transaction was declined, use the declined-card wording** — it offers help rather than asking for
money, which is the right framing when the customer probably thinks they have already paid.

Used within [collections calling process](/finance/collections-calling-process.md).

# Declined credit card

**Call:**

> Hello, this is **[your name]** with Natran Green Pest Control. I noticed you have an open balance on
> your account. It looks like your credit card was declined recently. I can help you get that fixed
> and make sure your service doesn't have any disruptions. Please call me back at
> **[your telephone number]**. Thanks and have a wonderful day.

**Text:**

> Hello, this is **[your first name]** from Natran Green Pest Control. I noticed that your recent
> credit card payment was declined. I wanted to reach out to see if there's any way I can assist you
> in updating your payment information. If you could please give me a call back at
> **[your telephone number]**, I would be more than happy to help you get it updated.
>
> You can also update your card online by visiting `natrangreen.pestportals.com` and find the option
> for Wallet. Let me know if you need assistance logging in. Thank you for your attention.

# Regular past-due balance

**Call:**

> Hello, this is **[your name]** with Natran Green Pest Control. I noticed you have an open balance on
> your account for some services we performed recently. I wanted to see if I could help you with
> payment. Can you give me a call back at **[your telephone number]**. I'd be happy to help you with
> this and answer any questions you may have. Thanks and have a wonderful day!

**Text:**

> Hello, this is **[your name]** from Natran Green Pest Control. I am reaching out in regards to an
> open balance on your account for services we recently provided. If you could please give me a call
> back at **[your telephone number]** I would be happy to assist you with making a payment and
> addressing any questions or concerns you may have.
>
> You can also make a payment online by visiting `natrangreen.pestportals.com`. To setup autopay or
> update the credit card we have on file find the Wallet section. Let me know if you need assistance.
> Thank you for your attention.

# Follow-up

**Text:**

> Hello, this is **[your name]** from Natran Green Pest Control. I'm following up on the open balance
> on your account. Did you need help making payment? Let me know if you have any questions. You can
> reach me at **[your phone number]**.

# Note the tension with the late fee rule

> Every text points the customer at the online portal. But
> [collections calling process](/finance/collections-calling-process.md) says to **advise the customer
> they will have to pay late fees if they pay online**, and that fees can be waived if payment is taken
> during the call.
>
> **The scripts do not mention this.** A customer who follows the text link pays more than one who
> calls back. Either the scripts should say so, or the fee rule should not depend on the payment
> channel.
