**Permissions may be required** to apply a credit.

# Steps

1. Open the **customer's card**.
2. Select the **Invoices** tab.
3. **Determine the total amount needing to be credited** — the invoice totals have to be added up
   manually.
4. **Add Payment.**
5. Select **Coupon / Credit**.
6. Enter the amount in the **Payment Amount** field, and again in **Confirm Amount**.
7. In the **Apply to First** dropdown, select each invoice the credit should be applied to.

> **The source is titled *How to waive late fees in Pestroutes* but contains no late-fee-specific
> step.** What it documents is the general coupon/credit mechanism — the same one used for the
> [11-month retention credit](/finance/retention-credit-11-month.md) and for
> [partial refunds](/finance/partial-payment-refunds.md). Waiving a late fee is one application of it.
> Titled here for what the content is, with the source title preserved above.

> **Nothing in this procedure records why the credit was given.** There is no note step, no reason
> code, no approval, and no limit — only a mention that permissions "may" be required. Fee waivers are
> therefore invisible to anyone reviewing the account later, and there is nothing to reconcile a
> waiver against [the monthly late-fee run](/finance/late-fees-and-interest.md). **Whoever owns
> receivables should decide whether a waiver needs a note and an approver.**
>
> **Partly answered in batch 4c.** A separate source documents eight approved coupon codes — see
> [approved coupon codes](/finance/coupon-codes.md) — so a *reason* is in fact recorded, in the
> Coupon Code field this procedure fills in at step 7. **The approver still is not.** Two of the
> codes assert that a manager approved the credit without naming which manager, when, or up to what
> value.
