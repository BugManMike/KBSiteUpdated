The duty list for the **A/R Collection and Payroll Assistance** role, titled *B&P Agent* in the
source — presumably Billing & Payroll.

> **This document predates the current systems.** It splits work between **ServiceCEO** and
> **Pestroutes**, running the same collections process twice in two systems. ServiceCEO appears
> **nowhere else in the corpus** — it is the pre-Pestroutes platform. **Treat the ServiceCEO steps as
> historical** and the Pestroutes ones as the ancestor of
> [accounts receivable daily operations](/finance/accounts-receivable-daily.md).
>
> Kept because it is the only document that states this role's shape end to end — including the
> scorecard and end-of-day reporting duties, which appear in no current procedure.

# Daily

**Start of day**

1. Clock in.

**Work orders — ServiceCEO** *(legacy)*

1. Receive daily work orders and close them out
2. Assign tech names
3. Send invoices
4. Process payments

**Payments**

1. Receive and enter payments
2. **Process credit cards for both Houston and Austin branches**
3. **Call declined credit cards**

**Collections — Pestroutes**

1. Pull new collections for both branches
2. Call to collect
3. **Create a task if you have not collected yet**
4. Follow up existing tasks, **note the activity** — left voicemail, spoke with customer — and
   **advance the due date to the next call**

**Collections — ServiceCEO** *(legacy)*

1. Print the collection report
2. Call to collect
3. Note on the account

**End of day**

1. **Update the tech scorecard**
2. **Update the lead closure scorecard**
3. **Complete the end-of-day report**
4. Clock out

# Weekly

- **Review Google Local Ads and accept or dispute**

# Monthly

- **15th — process the 11-Month Customer Credit**

> The source writes "15tth". The 11-month customer retention credit has its own procedure, covered in
> a later batch.

# What survived and what did not

**Survived**, in current form: declined-card handling and the batch/collections work — see
[accounts receivable daily operations](/finance/accounts-receivable-daily.md); the task-and-note
discipline — see [collections calling process](/finance/collections-calling-process.md).

**Has no current owner in the corpus:** the **tech scorecard**, the **lead closure scorecard**, the
**end-of-day report**, and **reviewing Google Local Ads**. Google Local Ads recording appears in the
[accounting schedule of events](/finance/accounting-schedule-of-events.md) as a monthly bookkeeping
entry, but **accepting or disputing** the ads is a different task and is documented nowhere else.

> Worth deciding whether those four duties still exist and who holds them. A role document that is
> four systems out of date is the wrong place for anyone to find out.
