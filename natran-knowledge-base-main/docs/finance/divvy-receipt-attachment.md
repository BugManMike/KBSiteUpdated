Divvy is the expense-management platform used to track and control company card spending. Receipt
capture is what makes a Divvy charge reconcilable.

# 1. Get the receipt out of email

Find the email holding the receipt and **download the attachment** — PDF, JPEG or PNG — to your
computer. If the receipt is inline rather than attached, capture it with a screenshot.

# 2. Find the transaction

In Divvy, go to **Transaction** and locate the transaction that corresponds to the receipt. **Check
that the details match** before attaching.

# 3. Attach it

Scroll to the bottom of the page → **Add Receipt** → select the saved file → attach it to the correct
transaction.
