Two vendors make up the **health benefit account in QuickBooks**:

| Vendor | Role |
|---|---|
| **United Health Care** | The current EPO/HMO network provider. |
| **Mutual of Omaha** | The current health insurance provider for **both APS and Natran** employees. |

The monthly cost of both is split across companies, departments and branches — see
[health benefit allocation](/finance/health-benefit-allocation.md).
