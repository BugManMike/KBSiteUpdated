# Who supplies what

**The company supplies:** shirts, hats and jackets.

**The employee supplies:** **khaki-coloured or navy-coloured pants** and **anti-slip shoes**.

> **Denim jeans are not allowed.**

# Footwear and ladder safety

Technicians who use ladders must wear shoes with:

- an **anti-slip rubber outsole**
- a **right-angled heel at least half an inch high**, for proper rung gripping when standing for
  long periods
- **safety grooves in the arch area** for added traction

Shoes must be worn **at all times during the normal course of work** and kept in proper condition.

> **If an employee cannot provide suitable footwear, the company may provide it at the employee's
> expense.** So the requirement is absolute; only the purchasing route is flexible.

General footwear rules: **closed-toe shoes or boots are mandatory for all employees.** Sandals,
flip-flops and open-toe shoes are not permitted, for safety reasons.

# Return and replacement

- Anything **bearing the company logo is company property** and must be returned on termination.
- **Uniforms must be returned within 24 hours** of termination or separation.
- **Missing, lost or damaged items are billable** and deducted from pay unless prior arrangements
  are made **in writing**.
- **Unreturned uniforms** are deducted from the **net** amount of the final paycheck.
- Replacement uniforms are billed **at market rates**.

Uniform deductions are one of the voluntary deduction categories in
[employment classification](/people/employment-classification.md), which requires written consent
before any deduction — worth reading together, since this policy treats billing as automatic.

# Appearance standards

**Uniforms:** worn by all employees on duty, **properly, with all pieces present**, not excessively
wrinkled or soiled, and **free from pests or other contaminants**.

**General:** neat, clean and professional at all times; clothing in good condition without tears,
stains or excessive wear. Office dress code is **casual but professional** — decent, clean and
respectful, not overly revealing or offensive.

**PPE:** as required by the job, **based on state regulations and label requirements** — gloves,
goggles, respirators or coveralls. In good condition and properly fitted.

**Hygiene and grooming:** clean and well-groomed hair, nails and body. **Fragrance use moderate**,
to avoid discomfort or allergic reactions. **Long hair secured away from the face and equipment.**

**Jewellery and accessories:** minimal, to prevent accidents or interference with work. **No large
dangling earrings or bracelets** that could catch in equipment. **Visible tattoos and piercings
kept to a minimum or covered where possible**; tattoos professional and respectful, without
offensive, discriminatory or explicit content.

**Weather:** dress appropriately — warm clothing in colder seasons, sun protection in warmer ones.

**Exceptions:** specialised tasks or hazardous environments may require deviations, in which case
specific attire and protective gear **will be provided** and must be worn as instructed.
