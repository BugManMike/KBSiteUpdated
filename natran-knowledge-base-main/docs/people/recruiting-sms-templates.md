Around 30 SMS templates, organised by funnel stage and by role. **Blanks are `___` in the source**
and are filled with the candidate's name or the position.

> **Most templates hard-code a recruiter's name** — predominantly "Morgan", with some for
> "Michelle" as hiring manager. Anyone else using them must change the name. Two templates are
> titled *"…for Michelle"*, so the ownership split was deliberate rather than accidental, but it
> makes the set brittle as staff change.

# The funnel these templates imply

The templates only make sense against the process they encode, which is not documented anywhere
else:

1. Recruiter makes first contact by SMS, usually off a resume from Indeed or a prior application.
2. Candidate expresses interest.
3. **Culture Index survey** — a 2-question survey — is sent, via **JazzHR**.
4. **The survey gates progression.** Several templates exist purely to chase it: the recruiter
   cannot send the resume to the hiring manager until it is done.
5. Interview scheduled, by an emailed link or **Calendly**.
6. Hiring manager phone interview.
7. Onboarding documents issued through **EOSG — Employers One Source Group**.

Candidates are also pointed at the **company Core Value video** and at job descriptions on
`careers.natran.com`.

# Stage templates

**First contact — generic**

> Good morning/afternoon ___! This is Morgan with Natran Green Pest Control. I was wanting to follow
> up with you to see if you are interested in our ___ position?

**Application status holding reply**

> We have received many resumes for this position. My hiring manager is still reviewing them. We
> will be reaching out to applicants that she would like to interview further.

**Interested candidate — scheduling**

> Wonderful! I'm glad to hear you're interested! I have just sent you an email where you can
> schedule an interview! The link is at the very bottom of the email, below my signature, and says:
> "Click here to schedule a time". If you do not see it in your Inbox, make sure to check your Spam
> folder. Please let me know once you have scheduled it so I can confirm your appointment.

An alternative uses Calendly directly. A follow-on message then introduces the survey:

> I have also sent you an email with a link to our Culture Index Survey. Please complete this survey
> prior to your interview. This survey helps us to get to know you better. Please note that the
> survey is brief and has only 2 questions.

**No-contact after a missed phone interview**

> ___, I tried calling you for our phone interview but the call went to your voicemail. Please call
> me back or let me know if another time works best.

**Chasing the Culture Index survey** — three variants, depending on whether the interview is already
scheduled, already happened, or not yet booked. The common thread:

> I would like to send your resume over to my hiring manager to review, but the survey would need to
> be completed first.

One variant adds a practical note: **"I also recommend you take it from a device with a large
screen."**

**Matching an old application to a new opening**

> I came across your resume from a previous application and noticed the survey you previously
> submitted matches the ___ position we have available!

# Role-specific first contacts

Templates exist for: Human Resource Manager, Accounts Receivable Clerk, Customer Service, Call
Centre, Marketing, generic Sales, Sales Operations Director, Senior Sales Manager (Inside), Inside
Sales Representative, Outside Sales Rep, Outside Sales Manager, Outside Sales Director, Commercial
Sales Representative, Commercial Sales Director, Commercial Pest Control, Field Service Manager,
Pest Control & Rodent Exclusion Specialist, Licensed Pest Control Technician, Pest Control & Rodent
Apprentice, and Austin D2D / Door-to-Door Sales.

Most follow one shape — *your experience looks like a great fit for the ___ position we have
available* — with role-specific additions:

- **Technician and apprentice roles** advertise **"a company vehicle, gas card, and benefits."**
- **Austin Outside Sales** states **"on target earnings for this position are $55k to $100k +"**.
- **Sales Operations Director / Senior Sales Manager** describe "providing support, guidance, and
  coaching to our sales and customer service team."
- **Commercial Sales Director** offers the chance to **"hire, train and develop your own sales
  team"** for a department being launched.
- **Field Service Manager** says the company is **"looking to hire our second Service Manager"**.

> Several of these describe a point-in-time state — a second service manager, a commercial
> department being launched, a President role "placed on hold". **They will read as current to
> anyone using the template.** Recruiting to review and date-stamp or retire them.

# Onboarding templates

> Morgan sent you an email with an attachment with instructions on how to register for your digital
> on-boarding and you will also get a link from EOSG - Employers One Source Group to complete your
> W2, Form I-9 and information to set up your direct deposit. Please complete it before your first
> day.

> Please remember to bring whichever items you use to complete your I-9 with you tomorrow on your
> first day! In order to fill out the documents you will need to "Register" through the link that is
> provided! Also, please save this website link, this will be the same site where you can view your
> pay stubs!

> **"This will be the same site where you can view your pay stubs"** points new hires at EOSG/PrismHR
> for payslips. [Apple device self-service setup](/it/devices/apple-device-self-service-setup.md)
> tells them **Gusto Wallet** is where paystubs live. Two answers to the same question on day one.
> See [attendance event logging](/people/attendance-event-logging.md), where the three-system problem
> is set out.

> The source says "W2" where **W-4** is presumably meant — a W-2 is issued by the employer at year
> end, not completed by a new hire alongside an I-9. Transcribed as found.
