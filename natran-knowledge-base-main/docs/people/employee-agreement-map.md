# Mapped, not extracted

**1.1 Employment Agreement** — position, term, effective date, at-will statement, 90-day
probation, compensation by reference to the attached Offer of Employment, choice of Texas law,
legal compliance and reporting, agency limits, performance/waiver, severability, privity, entire
agreement.

- **Arbitration.** Binding arbitration, one arbitrator, **Houston, Texas**, American Arbitration
  Association rules. Both parties **waive the right to bring the dispute to court**. Costs split
  **50/50**; the **prevailing party's attorneys' fees are paid by the non-prevailing party**.

**II. Seasonal Employment Policy** — no PTO accrual, no benefits beyond legal minimums, term
capped at **9 months** consecutive or across a 12-month period and **not exceeding 12 months** from
hire. Exceeding the limit subjects the employee to immediate termination. The company may convert
them to full-time at the end of the season.

**III. Photo Release** — irrevocable permission to use the employee's likeness in company
publications, print and digital. Likeness becomes company property, no compensation, no right to
inspect or approve, all claims released.

**IV. Employee Handbook Policy** — the employee should receive the handbook at the same time as
this agreement, is responsible for flagging if they did not, and must report suspected violations.
The company reserves the right to reinterpret and change policies at any time.

**2.8 Key Holder Policy** — office keys and cards remain company property, returned on termination
or request. No loaning, duplicating or unauthorised use. The employee is **responsible for the cost
of replacement keys, rekeying locks and associated losses**. Lockup procedures and forms must be
followed if they are last on the premises.

**Motor Vehicle Record Check and Records Release** — authorises a consumer report and investigative
consumer report covering name, motor vehicle records and license verification, for employment,
promotion, reassignment or retention. Valid **for the duration of employment**. The company will
supply a copy of the report and Fair Credit Reporting Act rights on request.

> This is the only place in the corpus that names the legal entity as **Natran Green Pest Control,
> Inc.** Elsewhere it is *Natran, LLC* — including on the Workers Compensation policy recorded in
> the root Insurance document. **Two entity names for one company.** Whoever handles filings should
> confirm which is correct on employment paperwork.

**Workers Compensation detail** — network doctor requirement, **Texas Mutual (844) 867-2338**,
definitions of urgent versus emergency care with examples, medical records release, and a
fraudulent claim notice carrying discipline up to termination and liability for damages.

# The compensation policy's other provisions

Beyond the production pay formulas, section I states:

- **Annual raises are discretionary** — based on performance, tenure and company financial
  strength, but "may or may not occur, are subjective, and are decided at the sole discretion of
  the Company."
- Pay structure itself is **on the offer letter**, not in this agreement.
- Paychecks **every other week on Friday**, with a **one-week processing period**.
- **$45 check cancellation fee** for a lost, stolen or damaged paycheck, deducted from pay.
- Technicians receive work orders **on paper or electronically** and must complete all forms,
  contracts and procedures; **failure delays their pay**.
- Nonexempt employees get **time and a half over 40 hours** and must track hours as directed.
