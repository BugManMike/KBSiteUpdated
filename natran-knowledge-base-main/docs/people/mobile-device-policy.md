**"Mobile device"** covers a mobile phone, cell phone, laptop, tablet, smart watch, GPS unit,
camera, **or any other electronic device or accessory issued to the employee.**

The stated purpose is limiting exposure to theft or disclosure of information, service disruption,
fraud and litigation.

# Privacy — there is none

- **All contents of a company mobile device are company property.**
- Information may be accessed **remotely, without prior request**, or manually on request by
  management.
- **All calls, SMS and MMS messages are logged and kept** for quality assurance.
- **"The Company makes no promises or guarantees of privacy on Company mobile devices."**

> This is the provision employees are most likely to be surprised by, and it is signed. It pairs
> with the Teramind monitoring on Windows devices — see
> [Teramind user monitoring](/it/devices/teramind-user-monitoring.md), where the agent is
> deliberately installed without notifying the user.

# Usage

- Company devices are for **work-related purposes only**.
- **Occasional and limited personal use may be permitted**, provided personal charges are
  identified, quantified and **reimbursed to the company** — possibly by paycheck deduction.
- Devices may be used **only by the assigned employee**. No other employee or third party without
  **prior written authorisation** from the appropriate manager.
- Use must be **lawful, ethical and efficient**.
- **The company may withdraw a device** from anyone it believes is not complying.
- Devices must be **kept charged and switched on during working hours**.
- **Only properly licensed software purchased or approved by the company** may be installed.

# Security

- The company **may install security programs allowing it to track, locate, wipe and erase devices
  remotely.**
- **Any attempt to circumvent, remove or modify a device's security violates the agreement** and
  subjects the employee to disciplinary action.
- **The employee is required to secure their device with a PIN code.**

The remote-wipe capability is what Jamf Now provides for Apple devices — see
[Apple device erase and unassign](/it/devices/apple-device-erase.md).
