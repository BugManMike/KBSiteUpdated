# Approval

**Prior approval is required from both the immediate supervisor and the HR department** before
bringing a pet to the workplace.

# Health and behaviour

- Pets must be **in good health, up to date on vaccinations, and free from parasites**.
- The employee must bring **a signed document from a licensed vet** showing all vaccines are
  current, **and the pet must pass a behaviour exam**.
- Pets must be **well-behaved, non-disruptive**, and must not pose a safety risk to employees or
  visitors.

# Where pets may go

- **Not** in common areas — **kitchens, cafeterias, restrooms**.
- They **must stay within the owner's designated workspace**.

# Owner responsibilities

- **Cleaning up**, including providing waste bags.
- Ensuring the pet does not cause **excessive distraction** to coworkers.
- **Liability for any damages or injuries** caused by the pet to employees, visitors or company
  property.

**The company will not be held liable for any harm or injury caused by an employee's pet.**

# Revocation

The company **reserves the right to revoke the privilege** if the employee fails to adhere to the
policy, or if the pet's presence becomes disruptive or poses a risk.

# Allergies

Employees with allergies or concerns should raise them with **HR, who will work to find a suitable
resolution**. The policy does not say how a conflict between a pet owner and an allergic colleague
is resolved, or whose interest prevails.
