Employees get a **75% discount on recurring services at one residential location**.

# Exclusions and limits

- **One residential location only.**
- **Recurring services only.**
- **Mosquito Misting Systems and Sentricon sites are charged at full price** — they are excluded
  from the discount entirely.
- All work is **subject to management approval**. The company reserves the right to refuse work
  for any reason.

# Process rules

- **Services must be completed using regular protocols.**
- **Technicians are excluded from servicing their own property.**
- The employee **and/or the head of household** for the service location must **sign an agreement
  for services** and agree to all normal terms and conditions.

> **Failure to follow these processes is treated as theft of service**, subject to discipline up to
> and including suspension or termination.

That framing is what makes the process rules matter as much as the discount. A technician quietly
servicing their own house at a discount is not a paperwork lapse under this policy.
