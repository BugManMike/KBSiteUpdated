Classification determines **benefits eligibility and overtime status**. The company may review or
change classifications at any time.

# Classifications

| Class | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Exempt** | Typically paid on a salary basis; **not** eligible for overtime pay |
| **Nonexempt** | Paid hourly; **eligible** for overtime pay |
| **Regular, full-time** | Not temporary, works a minimum of **30 hours weekly**, continuous status. Eligible for the full-time benefits package |
| **Regular, part-time** | Not temporary, regularly scheduled **under 30 but at least 20 hours weekly**, continuous status. Eligible for **some** benefits |
| **Temporary, full-time** | Interim replacement, supplement, or specific project, at full-time hours for a limited duration |
| **Temporary, part-time** | Same, at **under 30 hours weekly** for a limited duration |

> For both temporary classes: **employment beyond the initially stated period does not imply a
> change in status.** Becoming permanent is an explicit act, not something that happens by
> elapsed time.

**30 hours is the threshold that recurs across HR policy** — full-time status, benefits, and PTO
eligibility all key off it. See [PTO — office hourly](/people/pto-office-hourly.md).

Seasonal employment has its own terms in the Employee Agreement, including a **maximum of 9
months** served consecutively or across a 12-month period, and no PTO accrual. See
[employee agreement map](/people/employee-agreement-map.md).

# Initial employment period — 90 days

An employee **may be discharged at any time during this period** if their supervisor concludes
they are not progressing satisfactorily. It **may be extended** under appropriate circumstances.

At the end, the employee and supervisor may discuss performance. If satisfactory, the employee
continues as an **at-will** employee.

The 90-day period gates several things: **PTO availability**, **holiday pay eligibility**, and
**401k enrolment**.

# Hours of work

Schedules vary by position, season and customer need. Hours of operation are **subject to
change** for inclement weather, unexpected absences and scheduling issues. Starting and ending
times and total scheduled hours may vary; the company reserves the right to alter them at any
time.

# Timekeeping

Under the Fair Labor Standards Act, **all non-salaried employees must verify timecard accuracy**
for hours worked, meal periods, overtime, and absences for sickness, flex days, holidays and
vacation.

- Records completed **daily**, showing the beginning and end of each work period **including
  before and after meal periods**.
- Corrections require **both employee and management** verification plus a **time edit correction
  form**.
- **Failure to submit a correction form before the end of the pay period may delay or prevent
  timely payment.**

> **Falsifying time records** results in discipline up to unpaid suspension or termination.
> **Recording another employee's time is grounds for termination.** Off-the-clock or unrecorded
> work is prohibited.

## Technicians

- **Dispatching from home:** clock in **when job duties commence. Commuting is not a job duty.**
- **Starting from the office:** clock in at the designated start time at the office.
- **Clocking out:** at the end of the final appointment, **only after completing all work-related
  duties**. **Travel time from the last appointment to home is not work time.** Technicians must
  **check out with their manager** at the end of the shift.

# Overtime

- Overtime is **actual hours worked in excess of 40 in a single workweek**.
- Nonexempt employees are paid **1.5× their regular rate** for those hours.
- **Paid leave does not count toward work time** — holiday, PTO, bereavement and jury duty are
  excluded from overtime calculations.
- **All overtime must be approved in advance** by a supervisor or manager.
- Failure to work scheduled overtime, **or overtime worked without prior authorisation**, may
  result in corrective action.

# Pay deductions

**Mandatory:** as required by federal and state law.

**Court-ordered:** wage garnishments and child support orders, remitted directly to the designated
authorities.

**Voluntary, with the employee's explicit consent:**

- **Uniforms** — provision, maintenance or replacement
- **Equipment repairs or replacements** — company or customer equipment damaged or lost through the
  employee
- **Other employee-requested services**

> Employees **must be made aware of and consent to any deduction before it is implemented**, except
> court-ordered ones. **Written documentation of consent is required and must be kept on record.**
>
> The Employee Agreement contains a broad standing authorisation for equipment and vehicle-related
> deductions, with specific dollar figures. Whether that signed authorisation satisfies this
> written-consent requirement, or whether a separate per-deduction form is needed, is not stated.
> See [equipment and inventory responsibility](/operations/fleet/equipment-and-inventory-responsibility.md).

# Pay day

- **Bi-weekly.**
- If payday falls on a company holiday or weekend, paychecks are distributed **the preceding
  business day**.
- If absent on payday, arrange delivery with your supervisor or HR — **otherwise the check is held
  until you return**.
- Employees must **keep direct deposit information accurate** and update changes through the
  employee portal.
- Errors: notify your supervisor immediately. Except in emergencies, **adjustments appear on the
  next check**.
- **Lost check:** notify immediately. The company stops payment and reissues after confirming it
  was not cashed. **There is a $45.00 service charge.**

> The Employee Agreement states the same $45 figure as a "check cancellation fee" and adds that
> pay runs **every other week on Friday with a one-week processing period** — a detail the handbook
> omits.
