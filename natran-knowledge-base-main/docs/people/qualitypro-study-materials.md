A reading list for the three Public Health exams. **These are pointers to third-party material —
none of it is company content.** To register, see
[QualityPro certification registration](/people/qualitypro-certification-registration.md).

# Public Health Core Exam

**Books, manuals, guidelines**

- *Truman's Scientific Guide to Pest Management Operations*
- *The Mallis Handbook of Pest Control*
- *ACE Study Guide*
- University of Kentucky Public Health Pest Management Training Guide — `uky.edu/Ag/PAT/Ent_63.pdf`
- NPMA Field Guide App and *NPMA Field Guide to Structural Pests*

# Rodent Specialty Exam

**Books, manuals, guidelines**

- *Rodent Control: A Practical Guide for Pest Management Professionals* — Dr. Robert Corrigan
- Conducting Rodent Surveys — CDC, `cdc.gov/nceh/ehs/docs/IPM_Manual.pdf`
- Pest Prevention by Design — `sfenvironment.org/download/pest-prevention-by-design-guidelines`
- *Vertebrate Pest Handbook* — Dr. Austin Frishman
- *Veterinarian's Guide to Accidental Rodenticide Ingestion by Dogs and Cats* — LiphaTech
- *Full Revelations of a Professional Rat-Catcher After 25 Years' Experience* — Ike Matthews
- NPMA's Pest Management Standards for Food Processing & Handling Facilities

**Websites**

- `cdc.gov/rodents/`
- `npic.orst.edu/factsheets/rodenticides.html`
- `extension2.missouri.edu/g9446`

**Training**

- The rodent module of the CDC's Environmental Health Professional course
- A Rodent Control Academy — many are run around the country, based on the NYC Rodent Control
  Academy
- NPMA Rodenticide Stewardship Course, at `npmatraining.org`
- NPMA 360 videos with Bobby Corrigan — **best viewed on a phone**, for the 360 experience

# Mosquito Specialty Exam

**Books, manuals, guidelines**

- *Best Practices for Integrated Mosquito Management Manual*, 2017 — `mosquito.org/page/training`
- *Florida Mosquito Control 2018*
- *A Strategy for Integrating Best Practices with New Science to Prevent Disease Transmission by
  Aedes Mosquito Vectors*
- *Guidelines for the Emergency Management of Vector-Borne Disease Outbreaks*
- *Control of Communicable Diseases Manual* — APHA
- Coachella Valley District Mosquito & Vector Control, *District Invasive Mosquito Species Response
  Plan*
- AZ Arboviral Handbook for Chikungunya, Dengue and Zika Viruses
- **The owner's manuals and pesticide labels of all equipment and products you will use in your
  service**

**Websites**

- `wrbu.si.edu/aors/northcom_Keys.html`
- `vectormap.si.edu/`
- `nola.gov/mosquito/`
- `edis.ifas.ufl.edu/topic_family_culicidae`
- All CDC pages on mosquito management, including the West Nile vector control pages

**Training**

- New Orleans Mosquito, Termite and Rodent Control Board's Mosquito Academy
- *Zika Virus and Integrated Vector Management*, at `npmatraining.org`
- The mosquito portions of the CDC's Vector Control for Environmental Health Professionals course
- AMCA's online trainings

> The last bullet of the mosquito book list is the only company-specific item in the whole document,
> and it is the most practical: **read the labels and manuals for the kit you actually use.**

> This document covers only the three **Public Health** exams. It offers nothing for the
> **QualityPro US for Sales & Service Employees** or **GreenPro** exams, which are the two most
> employees take. Their study guides exist as PDFs in the wiki instead.
