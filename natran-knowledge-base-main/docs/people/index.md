# People

HR policy, compensation, hiring, onboarding, time off, safety and team processes.

Two signed documents sit behind most of this — the **Employee Handbook** and the **New Employee
Agreement**. Both are authoritative in their original form; the concepts here extract the
operational parts and map the rest.

## The signed documents

* [Employee Handbook map](employee-handbook-map.md) - What the handbook contains section by section, which parts have been extracted, and why the rest have not.
* [Employee Agreement map](employee-agreement-map.md) - What the agreement contains, which operational policies came out of it, and the legal terms left in place.
