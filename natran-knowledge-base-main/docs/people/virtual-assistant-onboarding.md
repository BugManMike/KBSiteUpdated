Virtual assistants are **foreign contract workers who invoice for their labour** — see
[employee expense accounts](/finance/employee-expense-accounts.md) for how each role is expensed.
This is what they complete **after the contract is signed**.

# Step 1. W-8BEN

The IRS form **W-8BEN** establishes foreign status and any tax-treaty benefit. Download it from the
IRS site and complete three parts:

- **Part I — beneficial owner.** Name, country of citizenship, residential and mailing address, the
  taxpayer identification number issued by their own tax authority (line 6), and date of birth.
  Mailing address may be left blank or marked *same as above* if identical.
- **Part II — treaty benefits.** Country of residence on line 9, then the article and paragraph of
  that country's treaty with the US in Part II, section 10.
- **Part III — certification.** Printed name, signature, date. **Read the certifications before
  signing.**

**Submit the completed form to HR.**

> **The source gives a filled-in example for Philippines-based VAs**, asserting a permanent
> establishment there on the basis that the employer is a US resident and all work is performed in
> the Philippines, per Article 5. **That is a tax position stated in an onboarding document with no
> sign-off from anyone qualified to give it**, and it is presented to the contractor as the answer
> to copy. The same paragraph is the one where a wrong entry creates a personal tax liability for
> the contractor. **Confirm with whoever advises on tax before this is handed to another VA.**

# Step 2. A US-dollar receiving account

The VA opens a **Wise** account to receive payment:

1. Register with a personal email address and select a **Personal Account**.
2. Select country of residence, enter a mobile number and verify the code.
3. Complete personal information and submit a valid ID for verification.
4. Verification typically takes **2–3 business days**.
5. From **Home**, open the **US Dollar** account, click the numbers under **USD Balance**, and copy
   the **account and routing numbers**.
6. Send those details to **accounts payable**, copying the **bookkeeper**.

> **The sign-up link in the source is one employee's personal Wise referral link.** Every VA onboarded
> through this document is routed through an individual's referral code, which pays that individual.
> Replaced here with a plain instruction. **The source needs the referral link removed.**

> **The VA is asked to send bank account and routing numbers by email** to two named individuals.
> No secure channel, no confirmation step, and the recipients are named personally rather than by
> role — so the instruction breaks silently when either changes job. No addresses or account details
> have been copied into this bundle.

# Invoicing

The VA sends a **PDF invoice on or before the submission schedule**, copying their manager:

| Contracted under | Send to |
|---|---|
| Natran LLC | Natran accounts payable |
| Advantage Pro Services | APS accounts payable |

> **The submission schedule and invoice format are referenced but not included** — the source says
> *"follow the schedule and invoice format below"* and then gives neither. A VA reading this document
> cannot tell when their invoice is due.

> **Cross-company again.** A VA may be contracted under either entity, and the document does not say
> who decides which. See [intercompany transactions](/finance/intercompany-transactions.md).
