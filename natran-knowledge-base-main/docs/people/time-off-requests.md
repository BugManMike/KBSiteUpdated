Time off is requested in the **Gusto employee account** on desktop, or the **Gusto Wallet mobile
app**. Requests are approved **first come, first served**.

> **Most of this document is Gusto's own support article**, lightly framed with company policy at
> the top; the source links back to Gusto's help centre. The 8-hour defaults and the tab names
> are Gusto's product behaviour, not Natran policy. Treat only the notice requirements as company
> rules — and see the conflict below.

> **If you cannot see the Time Off tab**, or cannot request time off, contact your direct
> supervisor.

# Company notice requirements

- Time off should be requested **at least 2 weeks in advance**, per the Company Handbook.
- **Unplanned** time off — emergencies or sick time — must be submitted **no later than the
  Monday before the next payroll**.
- Applies to **both exempt and nonexempt** employees, salaried and hourly.

> **This is the third of three conflicting notice rules in the corpus.** The handbook's attendance
> policy requires 2 hours' notice before a shift; both PTO policies say one hour. This says the
> Monday before payroll. They are answering the same question differently. All three are set out
> together in [attendance and punctuality](/people/attendance-and-punctuality.md).
>
> Note these are not straightforwardly reconcilable as "notify then submit" — the Monday-before-
> payroll rule can fall *after* the absence, while the 2-hour rule falls before it.

All requests remain subject to the general time off policy — see
[PTO — office hourly](/people/pto-office-hourly.md) and
[PTO — outside sales](/people/pto-outside-sales.md).

# Request on a computer

1. **Time Off** tab on the left.
2. If there are multiple policies, pick the one to request from.
3. **Request Time Off**.
4. Select the day(s).
   - **Full days** submit 8 hours per business day, weekends excluded.
   - **Partial days** default to 8 hours per date — **update the number as needed**.
5. Optionally add a note.
6. **Send Request**.

Payroll administrators are notified by email. The employee gets an email when it is approved or
declined.

> **If you need unpaid time off** and no unpaid policy exists in Gusto, ask your employer to
> record it. The five unpaid **flex days** in the PTO policies may be what this refers to, but the
> source does not connect them.

# Request in the mobile app

1. Open **Gusto Wallet**.
2. **Time/Work** tab. If it is absent, ask a payroll admin whether the feature is enabled.
3. **Request Time Off** → choose the policy → select the day(s) → optional note →
   **Submit Request**.

# Edit or cancel

**There is no edit.** Delete the request and create a new one.

1. **Time Off** tab → the relevant policy.
2. **View** next to the pending request.
3. **Delete Request**.

> **Once any part of the paid time off has been processed through payroll, the Delete Request
> option disappears.** After that, only a payroll administrator can adjust it.

# Reading your balance

**Time Off** tab → the policy. The figures shown:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Available Hours | Remaining hours you can use |
| Requested Hours | Pending + approved hours combined |
| Earning Policy | The rate at which you accrue |
| Maximum Hours | The annual cap, if your policy has one |
| Pending Requests | Requested, not yet approved or declined |
| Approved Requests | Approved but not yet paid through payroll |
| Declined | Requested and declined |
| Past Vacation/Sick Time | Approved and already paid through payroll |

> **Requested Hours combines pending and approved**, so it is not a measure of what is still
> awaiting a decision. Available Hours is the figure that matters for planning.
