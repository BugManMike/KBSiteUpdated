A comprehensive benefits plan is offered to **full-time employees** — see
[employment classification](/people/employment-classification.md) for the 30-hour threshold.

# Election window

- **Elect or decline within 30 days** of receiving the benefits package.
- **Declining locks you out until the next annual open enrollment.**
- **Accepting means you cannot cancel** unless a qualifying event occurs, per the healthcare
  agreement.

# Waiting periods

| Benefit | Starts |
|---|---|
| Health insurance | **First of the month following a 60-day waiting period** |
| 401k | **First of the month following a 90-day waiting period** |

- **If coverage is elected, deductions begin 30 days before the benefits are available to use.**
- Coverage **terminates at the end of the month** on termination of employment.
- Any insurance credits are **refunded on the final paycheck**.

> The deduction-before-coverage rule catches people: an employee sees a benefits deduction on a
> paycheck a month before they can use the plan. It is stated plainly in the agreement but is the
> kind of thing that generates payroll questions.

# Annual open enrollment calendar

From the HR wiki landing page. **Premiums are collected a month in advance in every case.**

| Opens | Plan | Due | Effective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **May 15** | MetLife insurance | June 15 | **August** |
| **August 1** | Health benefits | End of August | **October** |
| **September 1** | Guardian vision / dental | End of September | **November** |

> The source's third row reads *"Effective November in November"* — a duplicated word, transcribed
> as November.

> **Three carriers on three staggered calendars**, so there is no single open-enrollment moment.
> MetLife closes before health benefits even open. Anyone communicating "open enrollment" to staff
> needs to say which one.

> The calendar names MetLife and Guardian but not the health carrier. The accounting batch's
> health-benefit payment procedures reference **United Health Care** and **Mutual of Omaha**, which
> may fill that gap — flagged for cross-checking there.
