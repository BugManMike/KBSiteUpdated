**$2,000 (minus taxes)** for referring a candidate who is hired — paid across three milestones,
not up front.

| Milestone | Payment |
|---|---|
| Referred employee completes **30 days** | **$250** |
| Referred employee reaches **180 days** | **$750** |
| Referred employee's **1-year anniversary** | **$1,000** |

The three payments total the advertised $2,000.

> **Both people must still be employed when each payment falls due** — see below. So the headline
> figure is only realised if the referrer and the referred both stay a full year.

# Eligibility

- **Referrals must be emailed to Human Resources**, and the candidate must be **new**. If the
  company already has a record of the applicant, **the referral does not count**.
- **Temporary, contract and former employees** are not eligible for referral awards.
- **Both the referring employee and the referred candidate must be active employees at the time
  the bonus is paid.**
- **The first employee to refer a candidate is the only one eligible** for payment.
- **All employees are eligible except Human Resources personnel and managers with hiring authority
  over the referred candidate.**
- Only candidates meeting the **essential qualifications** for the position are considered.
- Hiring decision information **remains strictly confidential**.
- **Disputes and interpretations are handled through Human Resources.**

> "The candidate must be a new candidate" combined with "the first employee to refer" means timing
> decides the claim. Emailing HR is what establishes it — a verbal mention to a manager does not.
