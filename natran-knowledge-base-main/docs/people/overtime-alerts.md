Overtime alerts are configured **per department** in uAttend: which employees are watched on the
left, which administrators and supervisors are notified on the right.

# Add an employee to the alert

1. Sign in to **uAttend**.
2. Select **Departments**.
3. Open the **Alerts** dropdown and choose **Overtime**.
4. Overtime Alert settings appear grouped by department. **Click the department you need.**
5. In the employee list on the **left**, check each employee to include.
6. **Save.**

# Change who receives the alert

**Only uAttend administrators and supervisors can receive alerts.**

1. Sign in to **uAttend**.
2. **Departments → Alerts → Overtime.**
3. Click the department.
4. On the **right** is the list of admins and supervisors assigned to that department. Check the
   name that should receive alerts.
5. **Confirm the email address and telephone number are correct**, then **Save**.

> **New employees are not added to overtime alerts automatically.** Nothing in
> [the onboarding path](/people/employee-file-worksheet.md) or the
> [HR email templates](/people/hr-email-templates.md) includes this step, so an employee can work
> overtime unwatched until someone notices. It is a per-department checkbox that has to be
> remembered.

> **uAttend is reached on an Advantage Pro Services tenant**, not a Natran one — the same as in the
> [biweekly payroll checklist](/people/payroll-checklist.md).
