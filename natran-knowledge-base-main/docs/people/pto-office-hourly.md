**Effective 31 January 2022.** This is the "separate form" the Employee Handbook refers to when
it says detailed PTO provisions are given to eligible employees. Outside sales has its own,
different policy — see [PTO — outside sales](/people/pto-outside-sales.md).

This document is signed by the employee.

# Eligibility

- **Permanent** and scheduled to work **at least 30 hours per week** on a regular basis.
- Employees under 30 hours per week and **temporary seasonal employees do not accrue PTO or flex
  days**.
- If status changes from temporary to permanent, accrual begins at the date of that change.
- **PTO is available for use after the 90-day initial employment period.**

The accrual year starts on the employee's **date of hire**, not the calendar year.

# Accrual

| Years of service | Bi-weekly hours | Annual hours | Annual days | Unpaid flex days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–24 months | 2.1538 | 56 | 7 | 5 |
| 2 years | 3.0769 | 80 | 10 | 5 |
| 3 years | 3.4615 | 96 | 12 | 5 |
| 5+ years | 4.6153 | 120 | 15 | 5 |

- **Maximum carryover: 40 hours** of accrued unused PTO from one accrual year to the next.
- Paid at the employee's **normal hourly rate**. Eight hours equals one day's pay.
- **Excludes overtime, commissions and bonus compensation** from the calculation.
- May be taken in **4-hour increments**.

# Draw-ahead

An employee may request a draw of up to **3 days (24 hours)** against time not yet accrued. They
are then **ineligible for further PTO until the deficit is replenished**.

> **A negative PTO balance at separation is deducted from the final paycheck.**

# Flex days

**Five unpaid flex days** per year, in addition to accrued PTO. Intended for personal sickness,
family sickness, family activities, bereavement and extra holiday time.

- Available in 4-hour increments.
- **Do not carry over** between years.
- **May not be used to cover lateness.**
- Unpaid absences beyond five days "can be considered excessive, and grounds for disciplinary
  action."

# Requesting time off

Employees complete a **Time Off Request Form**, subject to managerial approval.

> **The form is superseded by Gusto.** See [time off requests](/people/time-off-requests.md).
> Both the handbook and both PTO policies still refer to a form.

**Notice:** notify management at least **one hour before the next shift**; a day's notice
preferred. For **multiple consecutive days, no less than two weeks in advance** of the first day.

> **One hour conflicts with the handbook's 2-hour rule** and with Gusto's Monday-before-payroll
> rule. See [attendance and punctuality](/people/attendance-and-punctuality.md), where all three
> are set out.

# Busy season

**Do not expect approval for time off during March – August.** Vacation during this window is
discouraged and handled case by case. Holiday requests are first come, first served.

> The handbook agrees on March–August. **The outside sales policy does not** — it names
> March–May and October–December.

# Cash-out and forfeiture

- PTO **may not** be used in lieu of proper notice of resignation, or during the two-week notice
  period.
- **May not** be used for periods of disciplinary suspension.
- Unused PTO can be **cashed out** with 2 weeks' notice, or **4 weeks' notice for key
  executives** (General/Branch Manager, Stockholder, Leadership Team Members).
- **On termination, PTO is forfeited.**

> Cash-out on notice but forfeiture on termination is a meaningful distinction that turns on how
> the employment ends. The policy does not define which departures count as "termination".
