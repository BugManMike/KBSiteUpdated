QualityPro and GreenPro are the company's industry certifications — per
[the Natran brand](/people/index.md), a standard held by "less than 1% of all pest management
providers." Exams are administered by the **NPMA** at `npmatraining.org`.

# Register

1. Visit `npmatraining.org`.
2. **Purchase New Content**.
3. **Login** or **Create a New Account**.
4. **Select Courses**.
5. **Add** the courses being pursued:
   - QualityPro US for Sales & Service Employees
   - GreenPro
   - QualityPro Public Health Core Exam
   - QualityPro Public Health Mosquito Specialty Exam
   - QualityPro Public Health Rodent Specialty Exam
6. Once all courses are in the order summary — **even if they cost $0** — click **Continue** under
   the summary.

That produces a confirmation screen and access to the exam site, where the candidate can reach the
**study guides, take the exams, and get their certificates**.

> **Step 6 is the one people miss.** A $0 course still has to go through checkout. An employee who
> adds a free course and then closes the page never gets access.

# Afterwards

Return to `npmatraining.org` and click **Access Purchased Content**.

# Trouble

Email **`training@pestworld.org`** and NPMA will assist.
