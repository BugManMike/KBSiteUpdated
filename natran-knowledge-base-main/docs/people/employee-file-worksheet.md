A single-page worksheet, titled *Employee File Worksheet* internally and *New Hire Worksheet* as a
filename. It spans the whole employment lifecycle — it opens with hire details and closes with a
separation date and reason.

**The blank form is the artifact.** The fields it collects:

# Identity and contact

- First name, middle name, last name
- Date of hire
- Home address
- Mobile number (personal)
- Date of birth
- Driver's licence number and state
- Social Security number
- Email address

# Licensing

- Licence application date, received date
- Licence number
- **Licence level:** Apprentice / Technician / CA / Termite

Those four levels are the Texas structural pest control licence tiers. See the Operations batch for
the TDA forms and the apprentice-to-technician progression.

# Onboarding milestones

- Training records complete date
- Uniforms and patches ordered date

# Pay

- **Rate of pay (training)**
- **Rate of pay (initial production)**

> Two distinct rates, which matches the production pay structure — a technician has a training rate
> before moving to a production percentage. See
> [technician production pay](/people/technician-production-pay.md).

# Separation

- Date of separation
- Reason
- Notes (free text, spanning the page)

# Two defects in the source

> **A real surname is left in the "Last name" field** of what is otherwise a blank template. Someone
> filled the form in, then cleared most of it before saving it back as the master. **Delete it** —
> anyone copying this template starts with a stranger's name on it.

> **The form collects Social Security numbers and dates of birth on paper.** This bundle records only
> that the field exists; no personal data has been transcribed — the repo's content rules forbid
> storing SSNs anywhere in it. That rule binds this bundle but obviously cannot bind a paper form.
> **Where completed copies live, and who can see them, is not documented anywhere.** Given the same
> four SSN digits are also used as the fuel PIN — see
> [HR email templates](/people/hr-email-templates.md) — that gap is worth closing.

> The worksheet also duplicates data that lives in Gusto and the EOSG/PrismHR portal. Whether it is
> still in use, or is a pre-Gusto artifact, is not stated. HR to confirm.
